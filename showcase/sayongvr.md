# SayongVR — A Virtual Labu Sayong Experience

SayongVR brings traditional Labu Sayong pottery into an immersive environment through virtual pottery, hand tracking, and interactive 3D modelling.

## 3D modelling

![3D pottery model in the SayongVR modelling environment](../assets/sayongvr-model.jpg)

*A pottery model shown in the project's 3D modelling environment.*

The modelling method, **Slice-Based Volumetric Modelling with Localized Deformation**, is the subject of **MyIPO patent application PI 2026005263 (patent filed)**.

## Research focus

- Immersive interaction with traditional pottery forms
- Hand tracking for interactive 3D modelling
- Slice-based volumetric modelling and localized deformation
