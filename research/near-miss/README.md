# Traffic tracking and near-miss analysis

This study examines vehicle detection and near-miss analysis under different CCTV video quality conditions. It compares detector variants and uses visual monitoring and distance-based indicators to investigate traffic interactions.

## Read the paper

**Near Miss Detection Using Distancing Monitoring and Distance-Based Proximal Indicators**

## How the research connects to the software

The private archive collects supervised detection/tracking research and Mathtech training materials, including YOLOv8 and DeepSORT variants. It has not been established as the exact implementation of the paper’s YOLOv7+CNeB experiments.

**Workflow:** Traffic video → vehicle detection and tracking → proximity indicators → candidate-event review

## Limits

Detection quality depends on footage and viewpoint. Proximity indicators support investigation; they do not by themselves establish collision risk or causation.

Cite the linked paper when using its findings or methods. For any software actually used, also credit the relevant implementation, version and upstream contributors.
