## Why researchers may reuse it

The survey provides an entry point into human-body representations, 2D pose-estimation stages, top-down and bottom-up pipelines, model components, keypoint formats, datasets and unresolved challenges.

## Practical selection checklist

When selecting a 2D pose model, report:

- single-person or multi-person setting;
- top-down, bottom-up or direct-regression pipeline;
- keypoint convention and dataset compatibility;
- occlusion, image-scale and crowding conditions;
- accuracy metric and computational cost;
- intended population and domain-shift risks;
- whether uncertainty or missing joints are retained.

## Distinct promotion sequence

1. **Method map:** top-down versus bottom-up pipelines.
2. **Limits:** occlusion, disabled populations, unusual viewpoints and domain shift.
3. **Reusable resource:** this selection checklist and the paper’s dataset/keypoint tables.
4. **Application bridge:** how 2D keypoints become reviewable posture geometry in [Human Pose Analytics](../../../showcase/human-pose.md).
