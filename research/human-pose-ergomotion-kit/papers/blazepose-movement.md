## Role in the research series

This established paper is the gateway into the newer 2D-pose survey, torso/spinal-movement work and monocular 3D pose for ergonomics.

## Reproducibility checklist

A related movement-estimation study should report:

- BlazePose/model version and landmark set;
- camera placement, resolution and frame rate;
- movement definition and participant/task characteristics;
- landmark filtering and missing-frame handling;
- reference sensor or motion-capture system;
- synchronization and coordinate alignment;
- per-joint or per-angle error, not only an overall score;
- limitations on clinical or workplace interpretation.
