# Enhanced 2D Joints for Spinal Movement — thesis resource page

## Canonical record

**Enhancing 2D Joints Estimation in Markerless Motion Capture for Improved Tracking of Spinal Movements**  
Ainun Syarafana Pauzi.  
Universiti Sains Malaysia thesis, September 2025.  
[Official USM repository record](https://erepo.usm.my/handle/123456789/24285)

## What the thesis investigates

The repository abstract describes two objectives:

1. compare OpenPose, MediaPipe BlazePose and MoveNet keypoints against inertial-measurement-unit data; and
2. enhance spinal representation with additional keypoints and Bezier/B-spline curve fitting.

## Promotion and reuse plan

- Share the official repository record rather than presenting the thesis as a journal paper.
- Create a visual comparison of the three model families using only verified thesis information.
- Explain why common skeletons provide limited spinal detail.

- Invite thesis-to-paper collaboration only after the student and supervisors agree on authorship, data rights and publication scope.
