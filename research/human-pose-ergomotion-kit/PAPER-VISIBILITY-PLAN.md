# Paper-by-paper visibility plan

This plan supports legitimate discovery and reuse. It does not request citations, promise h-index changes or encourage citation exchange.

## Current frontier context

At the 11 November 2026 Google Scholar snapshot, the author h-index was 22. Papers do not have individual h-indices; each has a citation count. Counts change and should be checked monthly.

## Distribution

- **LinkedIn:** plain-language visual and one discussion question.
- **ResearchGate:** technical summary, canonical record and companion checklist.
- **Academia.edu:** short research-interest post linking to the canonical paper.
- **ORCID/USM profile:** permanent links and verified metadata.
- **GitHub:** canonical companion pages and BibTeX.
- **Co-authors:** factual review and voluntary resharing; no citation request.

## Monthly measurement

Record the date, citation count for each paper, full-text or repository views where available, GitHub traffic and genuine reuse enquiries. Treat social engagement and scholarly citations as different outcomes.
